## Two bugs this check found (fixed in this build, re-measured above)
# 1. Landscape grid was off: Android does not swap DisplayMetrics xdpi/ydpi on rotation, so the app drew
#    the long (now horizontal) axis with the short axis's DPI. Before the fix the landscape capture measured
#    76.05 px across = 4.973 mm and 76.50 px down = 5.029 mm. MainActivity now swaps them at 90/270 degrees.
# 2. Portrait showed "98 bpm" next to "Checking signal": QualityRow took the pipeline, the same instance
#    every frame, so Compose skipped it and it stayed on its first state. Renders are first compositions
#    and could not show it. QualityRow now takes values; MonitorRecompositionTest fails on the old code.
