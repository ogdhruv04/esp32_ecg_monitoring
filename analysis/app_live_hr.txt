# Live heart rate on the phone: the 27 / 101 bpm bug and its fix (13 Nov 2026, detector tp3)

Seen on the phone (analysis/app_phone_replay_tb02.txt): the tb02 replay showed 27, then 101 bpm on a GOOD
signal about 30-40 s in, against a session heart rate of 77 bpm.

Cause: not warm-up (2 s) and not a gap (intervals across lost samples never enter the window). The
8-interval window exists to set the search-back threshold and has no age limit and no plausibility test.
tb02's first ~36 s are electrode-settling transients of +-1000-2000 counts against beats of ~40
(analysis/app_live_hr_tb02_beats.png, rows 1-3). The detector fires on them 2-3.4 s apart, and these
intervals stayed in the window for up to 25 s. Afterwards the threshold, raised by the transients, missed
beats and search-back added 250 ms double detections (row 4) - the 101 bpm. The quality gate called these
windows GOOD: kSQI rewards spikes.

Fix: LiveHeartRate.kt. Intervals between located beats whose later beat is in the last 10 s, none across
lost samples; good = 300-2000 ms and within 20% of the interval before (pan_tompkins.clean_nn bounds);
rate = 60000 / median(good) when >= 5 good, >= 75% of the window good, and the newest beat not overdue
(<= 2 x that median ago). The detector's own window is unchanged (it drives search-back; parity with
pan_tompkins.py holds).

"failures" = moments where the shown rate is more than 5 bpm from the reference (or from the loose
reference where one ectopic or missed beat leaves the strict one undefined), or shown with no reference.
The old display fails that check at the counts given. Rows 5-6 of the plot are tb02 at 140-164 s: clean,
regular beats about 620 ms apart, a real rise to ~95 bpm (reference highest 94.8 bpm at 156.75 s).

Not tested against the session heart rate, as asked: the live rate is more than 5 bpm from it at the
moments counted above, and at every one of them the independent reference agrees with the live rate
(within 5 bpm). A test pinned to the session rate would require the display to be wrong.

Guided breathing: its heart-rate number is the same EcgPipeline.heartRate (fixed with it); its beat-to-beat
curve (NN-cleaned intervals) was already clean: points more than 20% from the reference as counted above.
