Note on SampEn, written after seeing the table. On white noise the estimator settles: the
rule is met from the N shown above and no trial is undefined from N = 150. On pink and brown
noise the VALUE depends on N (r is a fraction of the sd, and the sd of a long-memory series
grows with its length), so there is no N at which a short window reproduces a long one.
That is a property of sample entropy, not noise in the estimate: a SampEn is comparable only
with another from a window of similar length, as with SDNN. config.json takes the white-noise
minimum for the template count and docs/hrv_minimum_windows.md records the length dependence.
