=== 1. Interrupt spacing, link test 29 Sep (no electrodes), on the ESP32 micros() clock ===
nominal 4 samples / 256 SPS = 15625 us. One gap = time between two consecutive FIFO interrupts.
ring: 11131 gaps; median 15625 us; 1st-99th percentile 15624-15626 us; min 15579 max 19532 us; within +-2 us of 15625: 98.9%
ref : 11136 gaps; median 15625 us; 1st-99th percentile 15623-15627 us; min 15620 max 15629 us; within +-2 us of 15625: 98.8%
The gap is timed by the ESP32's clock and the interrupt's latency, so this shows regular service, not the
MAX30003's sample rate: microsecond resolution cannot see ppm-level oscillator differences.
