WESAD evaluation (docs/wesad_plan.md sections 6-8), all 15 subjects, run once.
code fb1fa5c; config alg1; wesad/arousal blocks frozen 2026-11-16
amended 2026-11-23: both rule-3 (availability) amendments of docs/wesad_plan.md applied, approved before any classification metric was computed: ECG-time replaces ECG-full and BVP keeps only pr and ln_amp in the primary models; ECG-full is secondary analysis B3
Data: WESAD (Schmidt et al., ICMI 2018). DEVELOPMENT data for the PPG, EDA, temperature and arousal code;
leave-one-subject-out protects the classifier, not the feature code. The held-out test is our own protocol recordings.
Windows 180 s every 30 s, overlapping by 150 s and not independent: the subject is the unit of inference.
ECG causal monitoring filter, 50 Hz notch, tp3 detector at 256 Hz. Logistic regression, C 1, balanced class weights,
threshold 0.5; z-scores against each subject's reference half of the baseline; bootstrap over subjects, 2000 resamples, seed 20261116.
Stress is the positive class. Se = classified stress windows called stress; Sp = classified negative windows called
negative; P+ = windows called stress that were stress. acc and F1 (class-averaged, as Schmidt et al.) are over
CLASSIFIED windows only: read them with the rejection rates. A window is classified only when every feature of the
model was reported. Pooled values: value [95% interval, bootstrap over subjects].

== 7. Comparison with Schmidt et al. 2018 (plan section 8), and why it is loose
   Theirs (paper, Table 4; stress against baseline + amusement): best 93.12% accuracy, F1 91.47, LDA on all chest
   physiological signals (ECG, EDA, EMG, RESP, TEMP); wrist physiological signals at best 88.33% (random forest),
   86.46% with LDA; majority class 69.94%.
   Ours, B2, ECG       : accuracy 85.3%, F1 85.1, balanced accuracy 85.0%, on classified windows; rejected 0.0% of stress and 0.0% of non-stress windows
   Ours, B2, BVP       : accuracy 80.8%, F1 80.3, balanced accuracy 80.0%, on classified windows; rejected 13.1% of stress and 10.5% of non-stress windows
   Ours, B2, EDA       : accuracy 88.3%, F1 88.0, balanced accuracy 87.7%, on classified windows; rejected 0.0% of stress and 0.0% of non-stress windows
   Ours, B2, TEMP      : accuracy 65.5%, F1 64.4, balanced accuracy 64.3%, on classified windows; rejected 0.0% of stress and 0.0% of non-stress windows
   Ours, B2, Wrist-only: accuracy 90.4%, F1 90.2, balanced accuracy 90.0%, on classified windows; rejected 13.1% of stress and 10.5% of non-stress windows
   Ours, B2, All four  : accuracy 89.0%, F1 88.7, balanced accuracy 88.3%, on classified windows; rejected 13.1% of stress and 10.5% of non-stress windows
   Differences: their windows are 60 s shifted by 0.25 s, ours 180 s every 30 s; their baseline is all 20 min, ours
   only its second half (the first half is the reference and is never classified); we z-score against that
   reference, they describe no normalisation; we use no EMG or RESP; logistic regression, not LDA; we reject
   windows with a withheld feature, they reject none. Our 'All four' is chest ECG with wrist BVP, EDA and TEMP,
   neither of their sensor sets; our Wrist-only is the closest to their wrist result. A figure above or below
   theirs is not described as beating or missing their result.
